OmniRemote

One stick, every appliance.
一根棒控全家电器 — flash it from your browser.

USB-C-connect your M5StickS3 first.

Requires Chrome / Edge desktop (Safari and Firefox don't expose Web Serial).

What you get

  • Universal AC remote — 75+ brand/model variants (Gree, Midea, Haier, Daikin, Mitsubishi, Hitachi, Panasonic, Hisense, TCL, Samsung, LG, …)
  • Hold-A pairing — release when the AC reacts; the mic auto-detects the beep
  • One-button broadcast: short-press A toggles every saved AC at once
  • No WiFi, no cloud, no account, no app after this flash

How to use after flashing

  1. Unplug USB. The stick boots into pairing mode automatically on first run.
  2. Point it at an AC, hold A. The stick cycles through codes.
  3. The moment the AC beeps, the screen freezes on FOUND. Release A.
  4. Walk to the next AC, repeat. Up to 6 ACs can be saved.
  5. Daily use: short-press A to toggle, hold B-menu items for temp / mode.
