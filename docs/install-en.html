OmniRemote Web Installer

One M5Stack StickS3 controls every AC in your home. Flash OmniRemote firmware to your StickS3 directly from the browser — no PlatformIO, no Python, no esptool. After flashing it works offline forever: no WiFi, no account, no app.

  • Firmware v0.5.1
  • ESP32-S3
  • Offset 0x0
  • ~690 KB
Use desktop Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Open this page over HTTPS or localhost.

After flashing, unplug USB — the stick boots on its internal battery. If the screen stays dark, double-press the side button.

Re-flashing keeps your paired ACs by default: after you click Install, the browser shows an "Erase device" checkbox with Back / Next buttons. Leave it unchecked and hit Next — only the firmware partitions (bootloader / partitions / app) get rewritten; the NVS region is untouched, so every saved AC profile survives. Tick Erase only when you want a clean slate.

How to use

  1. Computer: open this page in desktop Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Hardware: connect the StickS3 with a data-capable USB-C cable.
  3. This page: click "Connect and flash OmniRemote". In the serial-port picker, choose the StickS3 USB CDC device.
  4. Hardware: after flashing, unplug USB. The screen shows the OmniRemote v0.3 splash.
  5. Pair an AC: point the stick at the AC, hold the front big button A (the rectangular one below the LCD with the blue accent). During SCAN the stick deliberately sends "Cool + 18 °C + Max fan + Turbo + Beep" so the AC reacts as loudly as possible. Release A the moment you hear the AC beep.
  6. Pairing auto-normalizes: after release, the firmware re-broadcasts your HOME defaults (26 °C / Auto fan), so the AC doesn't stay stuck at 18 °C / Max / Turbo. Screen returns to HOME.
  7. More ACs: walk to the next AC and just hold A again. No menu navigation — long-press A is the universal "pair a new device" gesture. Up to 6 ACs.
  8. Daily use: A short toggles every saved AC at once. The big number on HOME (e.g. 26) is the setpoint — what the next broadcast will set the temperature to, not a measured reading. B short scrolls the menu (temp ± / mode / fan); B long runs the selected item and broadcasts.

Speed up SCAN — only test your brands

By default SCAN walks all 83 built-in AC protocols (~80 s end to end). After flashing, open the configure page to tick only the brands you actually own at home. The stick will then sweep only those — usually a few seconds. Web Serial writes the bitmap straight to NVS, fully offline.

Troubleshooting

  • No port listed: try another USB-C cable (many are charge-only), and confirm desktop Chrome / Edge.
  • SCAN cycled 90 s without a hit: your AC may not be in the 75 built-in variants. Press B short while still holding A to lock the current code; or open a GitHub issue with your AC's brand & model.
  • AC reacted but the screen didn't auto-FOUND: move closer (< 1 m); or tap B short to lock manually.
  • CLI fallback: on a machine with PlatformIO, pio run -t upload -e m5sticks3-ac.
